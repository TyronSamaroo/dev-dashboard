01ProfileThe Reliability Issue

A reliability engineer who ships.

From 24x7 on-call at Capital One to a new AVP post at Synchrony, Tyron Samaroo has built a career on cloud systems that hold up in production — and a habit of shipping projects of his own.

Based Long Island, NYNow Synchrony, since Jun 2026Disciplines Reliability · Software · ML

TTyron Samaroo is a reliability engineer with more than six years of experience building high-scale, cloud-native microservices, ML pipelines and polished web experiences. His focus is reliability, event-driven architectures and shipping real projects.

In June 2026 he joined Synchrony as AVP, Reliability Engineer. Before that, from 2022 to 2026, he was a software engineer at Capital One, engineering high-scale backend services on AWS that powered millions of monthly requests — and owning production reliability, including 24x7 on-call support.

Earlier, at PicoPortal (IDOC), he worked as an ML engineer, automating an ML pipeline and building inference APIs with FastAPI. His first engineering post was at the Research Foundation of CUNY, where he built a Ruby on Rails admin tool serving 500+ users.

He holds an MS in Data Science & Engineering from the City College of New York and a BS in Computer Science, with a minor in Mathematics, from Queens College. He is AWS Certified as both a Developer and a Solutions Architect.

Off the clock, he builds: 8 projects shipped so far, with an iPhone activity companion, StrideSlip, currently on the bench.
