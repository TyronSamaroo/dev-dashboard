✅ Checklists

🗓️ Weekly ritual (every week until show)
  • Saturday morning: weigh-in fasted → check-in email to Bryan (weight, adherence, performance, stress, sleep, steps, bowel, photos)
  • Photos: keep Bryan's approved camera angle, same spot, lighting, and order — Front, First Side, Back, Final Side
  • Cardio: 6 days/week — training days 25 minutes fasted + 45 minutes later; rest days one 65-minute session; heart rate 140–150
  • Posing: 2–3 rounds × 60s holds daily-ish + 1 Josh or filmed session/week
  • Wednesday: gym progress photos
  • Non-negotiables: 1–1.5 gal water · 6.5–8 hrs sleep · pink salt with meals · 10k steps
🧳 Peak week (August 31 – September 5) — pending Bryan's protocol
  • Get written protocol from Bryan by about August 15: water taper, sodium plan, carb-up timing, training taper
  • Shave/trim schedule: rough pass Tuesday/Wednesday, final Thursday/Friday before tan
  • Spray tan: base coat Friday September 4, touch-up Saturday/Sunday per service
  • No new foods, no new supplements, no experiments
  • Pack Thursday: shorts ×2 (if possible), robe/slides, pump bands, music, registration + OCB card, cooler foods per protocol, rice cakes/honey, towel, phone tripod
  • Tiffany briefed: drive plan, cooler duty, filming every stage moment
🎪 Show day — Sunday September 6
  • Eat exactly per Bryan's day-of plan — nothing off-script until after the night show
  • Arrive at athlete check-in early; confirm class + lineup order
  • Tan touch-up → pump up 15–20 minutes out (push-ups, band work — no legs, no fatigue)
  • Backstage: sip water, stay warm, run the 4 quarter-turns in your head
  • On stage: slow transitions, breathe, SMILE — the 60-second holds made this easy
  • After: photos with Bryan/Tiffany, thank the judges, then the meal you've been thinking about since June 8
